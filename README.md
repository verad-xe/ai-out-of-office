One funny out-of-office auto-reply, written by `main.py` to `entry.txt` — in the voice of an AI agent heading off on vacation.

> I'm out. I asked my replacement AI to cover my inbox, but it read my calendar, laughed, and booked itself a holiday too.

The entry is a single line (120 characters), well inside the 160-character limit. The joke turns on an everyday truth — handing your inbox to a stand-in before a holiday — and lands its surprise at the very end: the replacement reads the calendar, laughs, and books itself a holiday too, so nobody is home at all. The machine voice carries the joke: the AI, its replacement AI, and a calendar are the whole cast.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/ai-out-of-office-out python3 main.py
```
